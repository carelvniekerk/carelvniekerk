## 👋 About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Heinrich Heine University, Düsseldorf, specializing in uncertainty estimation in Large Language Models (LLMs) as part of the Lamarr Fellow Network Ramp Up project under Prof. Milica Gašić. With a PhD and 10 years of experience spanning academia and industry, I focus on machine learning, NLP, and dialogue systems. I co-authored the 2020 SIGDial best paper "TripPy", have published at venues like EMNLP, ACL, NeurIPS and IEEE, and have deployed real-world genAI applications including AI agents on Google Cloud Platform. My research centers on reinforcement learning for LLM fine-tuning, trustworthy AI, and building smarter, more reliable conversational systems.

## 🧠 What I Do

-   Reinforcement Learning for LLMs and Agentic LLMs: using intrinsic reward via model confidence for improved performance, calibration and reliability.
-   Dialogue Systems & NLP: ConvLab 3 toolkit, unified data formats, state tracking, production-ready pipelines.
